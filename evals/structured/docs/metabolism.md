# Cellular Metabolism Overview

## Glycolysis

Glycolysis converts glucose into pyruvate in the cytoplasm, yielding a net of
two ATP and two NADH per glucose molecule. Hexokinase phosphorylates glucose
in the first committed step.

## Krebs Cycle

The Krebs cycle (citric acid cycle) oxidizes acetyl-CoA in the mitochondrial
matrix. Each turn produces three NADH, one FADH2 and one GTP (or ATP).

## Electron Transport

Oxidative phosphorylation couples the electron transport chain to ATP synthase.
Most cellular ATP is produced here under aerobic conditions.
