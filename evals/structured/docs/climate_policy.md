# Climate Policy Levers

## Carbon Pricing

A carbon tax sets a price per ton of CO2 equivalent. Cap-and-trade instead
limits total emissions and lets markets discover the price.

## Renewable Mandates

Renewable portfolio standards require utilities to source a rising share of
electricity from wind, solar or other renewables by target years.

## Adaptation Funding

Adaptation funds finance seawalls, drought-resistant crops and early-warning
systems in regions already facing climate impacts.
